# Hi, I'm Maximilian

AI engineer near Munich. At BayWa AG, I build LLM agents, document understanding and the group-wide AI platform. My research focus is computer vision that stays reliable when data and environment change – now applied to robotic manipulation.

Most of my professional work lives in company repositories; this profile shows research and personal projects.

## Robotics

- [**Vision pipeline for cobot sheet handling**](https://gitlab.com/maxistahl92/paper-vision-pipeline) – Grand Prize, robo.innovate Robotics & Physical AI Hackathon 2026 (TUM). Instance segmentation plus OpenCV geometry finds sheet position and angle in about 24 ms, also with overlapping or occluded sheets; includes hand detection for safety. [Full team project](https://gitlab.com/konukhovdmitryi1992/hackathon-durst-4b).
- [**SO-101 cube pick**](https://gitlab.com/maxistahl92/so101-cube-pick) – Pick task with an SO-101 arm.
- [**Robot motion template**](https://gitlab.com/maxistahl92/robot-motion-template) – Motion planning template for robot arms.
